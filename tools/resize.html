How to resize a photo for an online form

1. Choose your image. 2. Enter the width and height requested by the form. 3. Choose an accepted image format. 4. Resize and download your result. Always check the form's maximum file size and photo requirements before submitting.

Privacy by design

This tool uses your browser to process the selected image. The image is not intentionally uploaded to a FormPhoto Tools server. Avoid using public or shared devices for sensitive documents, and remember that downloaded files may remain on your device.
